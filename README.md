# Awesome Camping Power 🏕️⚡

A curated list of **free tools, calculators, datasets, guides, and communities** for planning off-grid power in RVs, campervans, tents, and overland rigs.

> Maintained by the [CampPowerCalc](https://camppowercalc.com/en/) team. Not affiliated with any brand listed here unless noted. PRs welcome — keep it free, relevant, and genuinely useful.

---

## 🔌 Calculators

- [CampPowerCalc — RV & Camping Power Calculator](https://camppowercalc.com/en/) — Interactive off-grid load sizing and solar / battery / inverter planning for vanlife, RV, and tent camping.
- [Van & RV Solar Sizing](https://camppowercalc.com/en/calculator/van-solar-sizing/) — Size roof panels from your real daily usage.
- [Camper Battery Size](https://camppowercalc.com/en/calculator/camper-battery-size/) — LiFePO4 / Ah sizing from the appliances you actually run.
- [Campervan Inverter](https://camppowercalc.com/en/calculator/campervan-inverter/) — Pick inverter size from your load, with surge headroom.
- [RV Electrical Load](https://camppowercalc.com/en/calculator/rv-electrical-load/) — Estimate daily watt-hours from each device.
- [Portable Power Station Runtime](https://camppowercalc.com/en/calculator/portable-power-station/) — How long a power station lasts for your setup.
- [Starlink Mobile Power](https://camppowercalc.com/en/calculator/starlink-mini-mobile/) — Power budget for running Starlink Mini off-grid.
- [RV Shore Power 30 vs 50 Amp](https://camppowercalc.com/en/calculator/rv-shore-power-30-vs-50-amp/) — What your hookup actually supports.
- [Weekend Tent Camping Power](https://camppowercalc.com/en/calculator/weekend-tent-camping/) — Lightweight setups for non-electric sites.
- [Vanlife Solar Generator](https://camppowercalc.com/en/calculator/vanlife-solar-generator/) — All-in-one station vs DIY build comparison.
