RV & Camping Appliance Power Database ⚡

Typical wattage, surge, and estimated daily energy for common off-grid devices.

Maintained by the CampPowerCalc team · Last updated: September 2026 · Free to cite with attribution.

How to read this table
  • Running (W) — typical draw while the device is actively operating.
  • Surge (W) — brief startup spike (compressor motors, AC); matters for inverter/soft-start sizing.
  • Daily Wh — estimated energy over a typical day of use. Fridges run 24h; cooktops run minutes.
  • Values are typical ranges from manufacturer specs and field reports. Always confirm against your unit's label before building a system.

🛠 Methodology & Sources

Figures are typical ranges compiled from manufacturer specification sheets and aggregated field reports (RV AC and Starlink data cross-checked against Starlink support documentation and multiple RV power draw teardowns; 12V compressor fridge duty-cycle data from BLUETTI, Jackery, and DIY solar community logs, 2024–2026). Real devices vary with ambient temperature, thermostat setting, age, and usage. Treat this as a planning baseline, not a substitute for measuring your own rig with a shunt or Kill-A-Watt.
